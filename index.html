How Rozgaar Saathi Works

Whether you need help at home or are looking for work — the process is the same: fast, transparent, and safe.

01

Search by skill

Select the type of worker you need — plumber, electrician, maid, cook — and browse verified profiles in your city.

02

Hire in one tap

Tap Hire. The worker gets notified instantly on their phone. No calls, no back-and-forth, no delays.

03

Confirm with OTP

When work is done, share your unique OTP with the worker to mark the job complete. Safe and verified every time.

01

Build your profile

Create a free profile with your skill, city, and photo. It takes under 2 minutes and makes you visible to nearby employers.

02

Get hired instantly

When an employer nearby selects you, you get a real-time notification with their name, address, and contact details.

03

Complete & grow

Finish the job, enter the employer's OTP, and build your rating. More ratings mean more hires and better opportunities.

Our story

We built this because finding a plumber shouldn't take three days

Rozgaar Saathi was born in Agartala when our founders realized that millions of skilled blue-collar workers had no reliable way to connect with nearby employers — and employers had no way to find verified help quickly.

We're a dedicated team of builders who believe technology should work for everyone, not just those with high-speed internet and expensive smartphones. We built this platform mobile-first, in regional languages, designed to work seamlessly on entry-level Android devices.
